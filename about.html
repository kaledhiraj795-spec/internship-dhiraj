A legacy with a future

Rooted in education.
Focused on what’s next.

Audyogik Shikshan Mandal (ASM) was established in 1983 with a commitment to preparing well-rounded professionals. Today, ASM Group of Institutes offers an education pathway from KG to Ph.D., helping learners build knowledge and confidence at every stage.

ASM NextGen Technical Campus in Talegaon Dabhade brings that educational legacy into a technical learning environment, with programs that connect academic foundations to the needs of a changing world.
